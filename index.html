VEHICLE PROCUREMENT

Vehicle recommendations

Vehicle rankings will appear here once the vehicle database is connected. The comparison will be split by vehicle class so that cars, vans, buses and trucks are compared like-for-like.

CarsVansBusesTrucks

The intended output is a top 10 within each selected class, ranked by estimated operational CO₂ per kilometre. Vehicle purchase or rental recommendations should also consider vehicle size, fuel, payload and intended use.

How the aircraft ranking is calculated

Each operator’s listed aircraft are evaluated at a standard 1,000 km flight distance using the ICAO fuel curves. The estimate uses reference seating capacity, 80% seat occupancy and a common 0.85 cargo allocation, then reports estimated operational CO₂ per passenger-kilometre. Operators are ranked by the mean of their eligible aircraft models; aircrafts without supported fuel curves are excluded. Actual results vary with route, load factor, cabin layout, aircraft age and configuration.

Because this is a standardised comparison rather than a route-specific calculation, use it to shortlist operators, not as a claim that one airline will always emit less on every route. The next update will compare operators on the actual route and the aircraft scheduled for that service.

04 / RECORDS

Flight records

Methods, boundaries & sources

Flights

Flight distance is calculated as great-circle distance from one airport to another with a detour correction added. Aircraft fuel curves are interpolated between distance bands. Aircraft seating and cabin allocation are populated from the project reference catalogue where a verified layout exists; model-level estimates are labelled when a specific layout is unavailable.

Route allocation follows the ICAO regional allocation framework used by the project. CO₂ is calculated from estimated fuel and a 3.16 kg CO₂ per kg fuel conversion as per the ICAO; non-CO₂ aviation effects such as contrails and NOₓ are excluded.

2025 Government GHG conversion factors for comapanies (cabin allocation)

Boats

Fuel-based combustion: litres used by the entire boat × the UK DESNZ 2026 petrol (100% mineral petrol) factor. CO₂: 2.33955 kg/L; CH₄: 0.00820 kg CO₂e/L ÷ 28; N₂O: 0.00597 kg CO₂e/L ÷ 265; total CO₂e: 2.35372 kg/L. These are direct-combustion factors; upstream fuel production is excluded.

The CH₄ and N₂O charts display the physical masses of these gases, not their CO₂e contributions. Factors use the AR5 100-year GWP basis of the UK 2026 publication. UK fuel composition may differ from Mozambican petrol; do not assume the UK biofuel-blend average applies.

Engine hours, passengers and personnel are operational metadata only. Do not multiply total boat petrol by engine count; exclude missing fuel and unresolved mixed boat/quad-bike fuel entries rather than imputing them.

UK government 2026 GHG conversion factors (full dataset and methodology)

E-bikes

Estimated electricity use is distance × 0.0405 kWh/km unless measured charging is supplied. Charging emissions are calculated from electricity × grid share × 0.226 kg CO₂/kWh. The boda-boda baseline is 0.058373626 kg CO₂/km and the selected replacement share is applied to that baseline.

For the project default, charging is treated as grid-powered from April–August 2025 and solar-powered from September 2025. Solar generation has zero operational emissions inside this accounting boundary; manufacture and lifecycle emissions are excluded.
