Electric motorcycles

Record charging, route and baseline-displacement information for the electric mobility programme.

01 / INTRODUCTION

Why electric motorcycles?

Electric motorcycles are a low-carbon alternative to petrol-powered motorcycles. Previously, C&C Rangers at the Masaai Mara relied on fuel-powered public transport motorcycles (boda-bodas) for site visits. The issue with public transport motorcycles was that they are not always available, and they are not always reliable, especially in remote areas of the forest. This made collecting seeds a challenge, leading to rangers being unable to collect seeds for the seedling hub. Rangers would have to walk long distances to collect seeds, sometimes being left stranded in the forest without a way to get back to the seedling hub. The electric motorcycles provided a reliable and low-carbon alternative to the boda-bodas, allowing rangers to travel to remote areas of the forest and education centres.

02 / PURPOSE

Why calculate them?

We calculate the emissions from electric motorcycles to understand the carbon footprint of our operations and to identify opportunities for reducing emissions. By tracking the emissions from electric motorcycles, we can compare them to the emissions from petrol-powered motorcycles and determine how much emission we avoided by relying on electric motorcycles, as well as the environmental benefits of using electric motorcycles. This information can be used to inform decision-making and to promote sustainable practices within our organisation.

03 / METHODOLOGY

How do we calculate emissions?

We calculate the emissions avoided by using electric motorcycles by first getting baseline emissions from petrol-powered motorcycles. We then calculate the emissions from electric motorcycles based on the electricity used to charge them. The difference between the two gives us the emissions avoided by using electric motorcycles. We use the UK GHG conversion factors for company reporting to calculate the emissions from petrol-powered motorcycles and Kenya's grid factor to calculate the emissions from electric motorcycles. We also take into account the distance travelled by the motorcycles, which allows us to calculate the emissions per kilometre travelled.

04 / INTERPRETATION

How should results be read?

The results should be interpreted as the total emissions avoided by using electric motorcycles instead of petrol-powered motorcycles. They are estimates based on the distance travelled by electric motorcycles. The emissions do not include the emissions from the production of the motorcycles or the solar and electricty infrastructure used to charge them. The results should promote the use of electric motorcycles to petrol-powered motorcycles.
