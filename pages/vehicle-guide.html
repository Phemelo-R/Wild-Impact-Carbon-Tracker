This pilot includes only the models and variants listed in Wild Impact’s current owned-fleet CSV. Each model family opens to its recorded variants. Replacement suggestions compare the same body type, since a motorcycle cannot substitute for a conservation fieldwork pick-up.

The fuel figures were supplied by taken from manufacturer specifications. Recommendations are screening comparisons, not procurement decisions: terrain, carrying capacity, purchase prices, maintenance and reliability remain relevant. Annual insurance can inform total ownership cost, but never enters CO₂ calculations.

Model specifications are public; importing the fleet CSV on the Vehicles page allows local asset counts and optional insurance figures to appear here.
